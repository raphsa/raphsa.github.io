# About

I'm Raffaele Sali, a Master's student of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Bologna.

I'm currently working on my thesis at the Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing lab at ETH Zurich, where I'm developing a state-of-the-art point tracker that remains reliable on non-Lambertian surfaces, as part of the research project **"Reliable Point Tracking in Presence of Non-Lambertian Surfaces"**, mainly focusing on exploiting Vision Transformer (ViT) and video Diffusion Transformers (video DiT).

I also fulfill the teaching assistant role in the following course:
- [**Applied Machine Learning advanced**](https://www.unibo.it/en/study/course-units-transferable-skills-moocs/course-unit-catalogue/course-unit/2024/504405), from the Department of Pharmacy and Biotechnology (FABIT), held by Professor **Daniele Bonacorsi**.

I fulfilled the teaching assistant role in the following courses:
- [**Big Data Applications**](https://www.unibo.it/en/study/course-units-transferable-skills-moocs/course-unit-catalogue/course-unit/2024/468888), from the Department of Economics, held by Professor **Matteo Barigozzi**.
- [**Informatics**](https://www.unibo.it/en/study/course-units-transferable-skills-moocs/course-unit-catalogue/course-unit/2024/403307), from the Department of Statistical Sciences "Paolo Fortunati", held by Professor **Stefano Pio Zingaro** (Italian-taught course).
- [**Econometrics**](https://www.unibo.it/en/study/course-units-transferable-skills-moocs/course-unit-catalogue/course-unit/2024/403286), from the Department of Statistical Sciences "Paolo Fortunati", held by Professor **Renata Bottazzi**.

My interests range across the fields of Reinforcement Learning for autonomous systems, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision for medical, environmental and automotive purposes.
